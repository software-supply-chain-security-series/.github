The **Software Supply Chain Security Series** is a series of the
[Joint Development Foundation](https://jointdevelopment.org/), part of the
Linux Foundation. Our mission is **to publish open standards for widely used
software supply chain security technologies.**

🌐 **Website:** <https://software-supply-chain-security-series.github.io/>

## Get in touch

Questions, ideas, or interested in bringing a project into the series? Reach
out in the `#software-supply-chain-security-series` channel on the
[OpenSSF Slack](https://slack.openssf.org/).

Organizations who would like to support the Working Group are encouraged to
join the [Software Supply Chain Security Series](https://join.sscss.io/).
